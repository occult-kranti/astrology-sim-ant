# Orbital Space — compare origins, inspect events

2026-11-02. Baseline c3ef4f00a984b23e976a3558b375db7a6d6b212b. Design Partner redesign/interaction in the existing static Calendar Atlas.

The immediate job is to select two bodies, understand their arrangement from Earth or from the Sun, locate an astronomical event, and compare the named calendar/chart rules at an explicit instant. The domain artifact is calculated geometry and a bounded event inventory. The dominant pattern is exploration with an adjacent measurement inspector.

## Direction and invariants

- Extend the black/neon instrument identity with restrained coral/amber/cyan structural rails, planet-specific colors and redundant event glyphs/labels. Preserve all existing atlas modes and calendar/reading functions.
- Earth-view uses apparent geocentric directions and distinguishes true 3D angle from longitude separation. Sun-view uses geometric J2000 ecliptic AU vectors and allows Earth as a pair endpoint. Origin bodies cannot be their own direction endpoints.
- Grid/symbol scale is explicit. Earth-view directions do not imply physical planet distances. Sun-view linear/compressed scales preserve unmodified source vectors. Display turns and tilts never change calculated facts.
- Timeline markers distinguish eclipses, pair longitude contacts, lunar phases and solar quarters through hue, shape, wording and separate filters. Major events remain usable through native selects/buttons/range controls, keyboard focus and touch; hover is supplementary.
- A selected event retains exact solved UTC, source method and limits. Casting is explicit. Personal outcomes and local eclipse visibility are not inferred from global astronomy.
- Favorable/caution classifications come only from the existing named rules at the stated reading instant. Their calculation/source evidence remains separate from event types and physical geometry. No universal good/bad event score.
- Static local modules; no new frontend dependency, provider request or database requirement. Public bookmarks require deliberate saving and exclude observers, birth records and secrets. Rebuild the existing offline manifest and RAG corpus through their generators.
- Stale input, superseded searches, cancellation and destruction must disable relevant actions and discard late results. Original deep chart and Sun–Moon plate remain available.

## Verification

Numerical checks must cover canonical vectors and angles, eclipse fixtures, root residuals, bounds, frame origin rejection, end-exclusive windows and cancellation. Browser checks must exercise actual frames/pairs, projection controls, event search/filter/selection/cast, bookmarks, source readings, stale state, SVG exports and 320/390/1440px layouts. Existing sky assertions migrate to actual visible replacement controls while retaining geometry and export checks. Record actual screenshots/download hashes and remaining limitations; do not claim physical-device or screen-reader validation.
