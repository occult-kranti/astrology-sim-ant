# Three rounds: synchronized chart and calendar timeline

Baseline: published `ae94fb003f00f3456635121e81f6616a6d6118a3`. Six specialist AI agents worked with the root integrator: engine, interface/worker, historical sources, study methods/MCP, independent QA, and independent source/numerical/performance review. These roles are not human professional endorsement, religious authority or observed participant research.

## Round 1 — contract, source scope and implementation

The team chose one shared civil-date cursor with explicit observer, clock, zone and house convention. The lightweight grid records clock availability; rich charts are calculated only for the selected date. Both UTC and local support remain1900–2100, with at most3660inclusive dates per window. Canonical calendar, sky and house engines are reused. Private births, personal questions, military objectives and country forecasts are outside this interface.

Historical research supplied twelve past records and eleven sources. Signatures, approvals and legal entry into force were encoded separately. Date-only is a property of this catalogue, not proof that every historical clock is permanently unknowable. Five peaceful task cards reuse three existing editorial profiles or explicitly retain an unimplemented method. Calendar labels do not acquire task scores. Source reading reused by the methods lane is labelled inherited; fresh historical research has its own acquisition receipts.

Root wired navigation, the source country-page entry, catalogue, MCP discovery, service-worker version and CI. Source presence does not establish hosted MCP or provider inference. The new page remains a study interface; astronomical event calculations, past historical assertions and traditional classifications do not predict wars, treaties or successful actions.

## Limits and telemetry

Software agreement does not establish historical timezone truth, source authority or predictive efficacy. The catalogue is selected, not exhaustive. Window scans, optional stars, civil calendars, sunrise schedules and historical source markers retain distinct methods and bounds. Dialogue and ceremony timing remain unimplemented. The timeline does not optimize military action or rank future national outcomes.

Telemetry claim counts are hand-classified coarse groups and explicitly unverified: six domain groups (armistice meaning; UN signature/force distinction; Simla dated agreement; Egypt–Israel signature/force; Israel–Jordan variants/stages; Belfast/multiparty versus bilateral legal stages) and seven tooling groups (grid/frame engine; charts/calendar presentation; worker state and exports; source/task presentation; MCP contracts; independent verification; integration/release binding). Actual numerical gates are parsed from executed logs and bound receipts.
