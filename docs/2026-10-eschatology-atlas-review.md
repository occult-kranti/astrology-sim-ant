# Independent eschatology evidence atlas review

This review concerns the new evidence atlas on `feat/eschatology-evidence-atlas`. It is an independent AI source-method and engineering review, with shared task context; it is not blind verification, human historian/practitioner endorsement or a new independent documentary source. The prior Newton release remains separately held and its live runner binds production responses to the supplied Git commit.

The reviewer owns this report and the two new independent atlas test scripts. Product and source edits belong to their owners. Evidence is retained outside the repository at `/workspace/setup-validation/eschatology-atlas/quality/`.

## Round 1 — source and acceptance boundaries

The Investigative Evidence Research skill and its evidence contract were read alongside the repository specialist/source and data-module rules. The panel workflow requires three rounds against successively changed artifacts, with failed diagnostics retained. This first round precedes the new data and runtime implementation.

Selected raw Pike/Carr/Taxil packet loci were read directly. A rendered Carr webprint page was visually inspected. The source hashes, reading limits and image transformation appear in `round1/source-review.json`. No full-book, original-manuscript or independent British Museum correspondence review is claimed.

| Finding | Required treatment |
|---|---|
| Carr narrates the three-war scheme before introducing a separate alleged-letter quotation. | Preserve the quotation boundary and attributed-allegation status; no authenticated Pike quotation claim for the entire narrative. |
| The retained *Satan* webprint page 5 prints June 1966 and “thirty years” after the preceding preface signed 2 June 1966. | Preserve the internal conflict; do not silently repair to 1996 or invent a publication date. |
| The BCY rebuttal’s museum-possession assertion footnotes the ThreeWorldWars website, rather than a retained direct museum response. | Keep the report attributed and its dependence explicit; do not count it as an independent museum record. |
| Gutenberg’s wrapper combines 1871/1906 copyrights and a 1944 reprint; its preface describes compilation and permits dissent. | Preserve the accessed edition and institutional scope, without making a universal present-day creed claim. |
| The translated Taxil admission names a distinct July 1889 circular. | Do not treat that admission alone as proof of authorship of the alleged 1871 letter or Carr’s three-war narrative. |

The six date roles are `claimedDocument`, `statement`, `publication`, `transmission`, `target` and `historicalContext`. Exact documented days, partial years, intervals and unknown dates remain different values. A document’s alleged date does not become a verified event date. Only a documented exact day within the canonical range may produce a source-date calculation. An independent study-date calculation must retain its explicit assumed clock, observer and zone while leaving source metadata unchanged. Historical BCE context remains typed historical numbering and is never coerced through JavaScript Date or year zero.

Jewish Kabbalah, its attributed historical adaptations, ancient Baal/title/deity contexts and specifically named modern Satanic organizations are different categories. Network edges must carry a typed relation, exact claim/source evidence and limits; a connection, repeated vocabulary or shared citation does not establish coordination, causality, common religious identity or prediction.

Runtime acceptance will require strict request validation before property access, unavailable/source-only geometry for unsupported dates, all 13 canonical civil-calendar rows and exactly 3 named readings at one explicit instant, source closure, truthful actual JSON/CSV downloads, keyboard/filter/graph access, revision-safe asynchronous behavior and 320/390/1440/1920 layouts. The inherited Newton 2060 result remains conditional year arithmetic; it supplies no exact target day. No national hidden-belief classifier, outcome probability, military timing or whole-day quality ranking is part of this atlas.
