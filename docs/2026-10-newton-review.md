## Round 1 — baseline and acceptance criteria

Repository verification, accuracy and shared source-contract instructions were read. Existing country-analysis results were inspected: 28 records, 23 source groups and 22 conservative analysis groups; 1,488 logical control uses over 1,323 unique samples. Those counts do not establish independent observations. Event clocks remain assumed, and the Jyotiṣa primary comparison has no complete nine-sample pairs. No pre-event outcome classifier, accuracy estimate or valid inferential p-value exists.

The inverse problem is not identifiable from these data: deterministic calendar labels and sky measurements do not observe a country’s secret beliefs, ritual choice or deity adoption, and no independently specified forward model maps those latent claims to the recorded outcomes. Shared solar/lunar derivations are not additional witnesses. Failure to identify a latent cause is also not evidence proving every possible association absent. The integrated interface must keep these limits beside the descriptive feature table.

Selected Newton Project transcriptions were read at the relevant 2060 and alternative-bound passages, with retained HTML checked for paragraph IDs and editorial deletion state. This is not complete-book reading or independent manuscript-image paleography. The evidence files and hashes appear in `quality/round1/baseline-review.json`.

The corrected independent semantic run passed 122 checks in `quality/round2/semantic.json`, including 365 date slots, 364 available samples and all 13 unavailable calendar entries on the skipped date.

The failed receipt and exact gzip-retained downloads remain in `quality/round2/browser/`; they are not relabeled as acceptance. These are not product defects or accepted runs.

Other independent panel findings led to visible unresolved-clock marks in both circular and linear instruments, quote-view and anchor attribution beside the claim, explicit signed feature-index units, and atomic staging of a new year until its selected frame is accepted.
