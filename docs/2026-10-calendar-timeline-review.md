## Selected independent source reading

The source lane retrieved the documents; the reviewer independently read the selected passages before inspecting encoded records. This distinguishes an independent reading from an independently originated source.

- Library of Congress, “Today in History – June 28,” supports the Versailles signature on 28 June 1919. Its compressed “formally ending World War I” summary should not become a universal legal-effect date, especially since the page also distinguishes later United States action.
- GOV.UK, “The Belfast Agreement,” supports signing on 10 April 1998 and separately records voter approval on 22 May 1998 and entry into force on 2 December 1999.
- Avalon, Egypt–Israel Treaty, heading and Articles I/IX: 26 March 1979 is the signature reference; entry into force and termination of the state of war depend on exchange of ratifications.
- Avalon, Israel–Jordan Treaty, heading and ratification provision: 26 October 1994 is the signature reference; legal effect depends on exchange. The closing Hebrew year is discrepant.
- UN Treaty Series volume 2042, I-35325, printed page 351 (PDF page 375): signature 26 October 1994; entry into force 10 November 1994 by exchange of ratifications; registration 11 November 1998. Printed page 403 (PDF page 427), Article 27 and closing attestation: Hebrew year **5755**, with Jumada Al-Ula 21, 1415 and 26 October 1994. The reviewer visually inspected the rendered facsimile pages. These are the treaty's recorded original labels, not claims that the application's civil converters reproduce every original religious boundary.

These timings describe this runtime and workload, not browser responsiveness or a portable performance guarantee.
