# Eschatology evidence atlas: methods and interpretation

This page compares a bounded collection of attributed claims, documents and calendar conventions. It is a source-critical catalogue, not a census of secret societies or a method for predicting wars. The named traditions are heterogeneous: Jewish Kabbalah, Christian and Hermetic adaptations, ancient Ugaritic Baal literature, Freemasonry, Thelema, the Church of Satan and the Satanic Temple are not one religion or organization. Newton's prophetic chronology is a separate Christian textual argument.

## What each view represents

The relationship map contains identified entities, claims and sources. Curated links retain a named type, asserted/disputed status, supporting claim identifiers and source identifiers. Comparison links are editorial comparisons. Automatically generated `catalogue-index` links mean only that a record concerns an entity or cites a source; they explicitly do not assert a historical relationship. Spatial distance, color, node degree and apparent clustering have no evidential weight. There is no graph-derived affiliation classifier or hidden-coordination score.

The date axis and ledger retain six distinct roles: claimed document date, statement date, publication date, transmission date, target date and historical context. A publication or webprint is not the date of the described event. Unknown fields are null. A year stays a year, a range stays a range, and historical BCE numbering has no year zero. An approximate point or interval on the visual axis is a navigation aid, not an authenticated event instant. Source reading depth, edition uncertainty and source dependence remain attached to the underlying records.

The ledger supports filtering and sorting. Evidence JSON retains the canonical catalogue and the applied view manifest; the visible ledger export and map metadata identify the exact selected records and filters. A filtered edge must retain every supporting claim or be withheld in full. Source counts count catalogue records, not independent witnesses. Inspect the export's scope before combining it with other data.

## Pike and Newton: different questions

The alleged 15 August 1871 Pike–Mazzini letter is not authenticated by the later reproductions inspected here. Its asserted document date is not a date for World War III. The inspected Carr narrative and later circulating quotation do not have identical quotation boundaries; the source ledger records this transmission problem. Reports about a museum response remain attributed reports where the correspondence itself was not acquired. Repeated citations to the same underlying web reproduction are not independent confirmation. These findings are bounded to the inspected source set and do not claim a complete archival search.

Newton's conditional **800 + 1260 = 2060** lower-bound argument and separate **70 + 2300 = 2370** upper-bound argument retain their own periods, sources and qualifications. They do not form a 2060–2370 forecast interval. The manuscript's unfinished and contradictory variants remain visible in the existing chronology tool. Neither argument identifies a precise modern civil day or clock. The separate Newton page supports exploring a chosen day or supported year; it cannot recover a day absent from the text.

## What the calendar instrument calculates

An eligible source date must be an exact, documented Gregorian or Julian civil day. Julian dates are explicitly converted before entering the existing engine. A claimed, conditional, disputed, undated, partial or unsupported source date stays source-only. The application does not fill missing month/day fields with January 1. A separately selected study date is permitted independently of the source's authenticity, and is labelled as that user's assumption.

Every calculated sample requires an explicit local clock, IANA zone, observer coordinates, tropical house system and repeated-clock policy. A modern observer and assumed noon are not recovered historical facts. The canonical supported interval is 1900–2100; early texts and the year 2370 remain readable without out-of-range geometry. A skipped clock remains unavailable. A repeated clock requires the selected disambiguation. Runtime time-zone data does not independently establish historic or future civil legislation, and coordinates are not checked against a geographic zone database.

For an available sample, the existing Newton exploration frame supplies canonical astronomy and **thirteen civil-calendar rows**. These labels do not provide thirteen independent prophecies or full validation of every calendar family. The selected instant is also passed to **three named inherited reading lenses: Lilly, Agrippa and Jyotiṣa**, for the Moon and the purpose `study`. Each reading exposes its measured inputs, named rule, source locator and limits. No additional Baal, Satanic or Kabbalah prediction engine is invented. The existing Era Legis calculator is linked for relevant Thelemic records as a separate tool, not executed as an extra reading.

Astronomical position, calendar convention, traditional interpretation and source assessment answer different questions. Shared Sun/Moon calculations are dependencies, not independent confirmations. Astrological interpretations have no demonstrated predictive validity for the political or eschatological events discussed. There is no combined good/bad ranking, prophecy probability, national belief inference or military objective in this page or its tools.

## Reproducibility and delivery

The pure core composes the existing canonical calculation functions; the UI worker keeps calculation work separate from document exploration. Changing calculation inputs invalidates dependent exports and contextual links until a replacement result is accepted. JSON and CSV reading exports serialize held results through the same canonical serializers. Cancellation cannot silently replace an accepted result.

Two strict read-only MCP tools expose the catalogue and one explicit source/study selection. Unknown fields, inherited/accessor properties, malformed dates and unsupported choices are rejected. Complete output has a disclosed byte bound; it is not silently truncated. Local SDK HTTP-handler and child-process stdio tests do not establish a hosted MCP deployment, provider inference or model training. Static GitHub Pages serves the frontend.

Source downloads and inspected scans are retained outside the product repository for research verification. The committed modules contain factual metadata, paraphrases, short selected quotations and source locators; modern editions retain their rights. Source hashes identify the acquired object, not its historical authenticity. The evidence and review reports distinguish independent research, implementation tests, browser inspection and actual release verification.
