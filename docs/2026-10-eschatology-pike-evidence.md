## Findings that must remain adjacent in the interface

1. **Quotation boundary.** Carr's three-war paragraphs are narration in the inspected webprint. The following sentence introduces the “We shall unleash…” block as alleged letter words. A later circulating form gives the impression that the whole narrative is a quotation. The module does not concatenate those forms into authenticated Pike text.
2. **Reported archival response.** The *Satan* reproduction's main text says the letter is catalogued; its footnote reports that the Keeper said it is **not** catalogued. This establishes an internal contradiction and an attributed report. It does not establish direct independent British Museum verification.
3. **A different Taxil circular.** The translated admission says he wrote the circular dated1889-07-14 in Paris. The module records this specific admission while leaving the1871 letter's authorship unresolved. It neither universalizes the admission nor treats every repeated blog or institutional assertion as another witness.
4. **Actual book context.** The inspected preface permits rejection of what seems untrue or unsound and describes compiled/intermingled material. Printed p.321 names the Apocalypse, Sohar and Sephar Yezirah; its Lucifer discussion includes the surrounding description of faith aspiring to God and despising Lucifer's works. These are bounded textual observations, not a secret-belief inference.
5. **Dates belong to different objects.** Alleged letter date1871-08-15; alleged circular date1889-07-14; reported conference1897-04-19; original report1897-05-01; translation1997; preface1966-06-02; later webprint2006; webpage update2007-05-17. An alleged document date is not a predicted event date.
6. **Target precision.** The inspected passages give no exact WW3year or day. Carr's separate claim that the alleged blueprint would reach its final stage “during the twentieth century” is preserved as a **claimed1901–2000 range**, with its limited referent. It is neither an authenticated Pike deadline nor a precise third-war prediction. Unknown target dates remain null.

These dates identify transmissions **located in the inspected packet**; they do not establish the first-ever publication of an allegation. The cited nineteenth-century documents and first Carr editions were not all acquired, so the module does not announce a complete genealogy.
