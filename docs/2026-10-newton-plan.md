# Newton chronology and historical calendar comparison

Work begins on `feat/newton-calendar-chronology` from published `b7793a47d34e048ebd8f06c8a3998864d5136e13`. The existing calculators, sixteen country founding/status records and their uncertainty remain available.

## User tasks

1. Choose a documented Newton chronology, inspect its symbolic interpretation, historical anchor, arithmetic and qualification, then explore a deliberately selected day in the resulting year.
2. Compare supported civil calendars and calculated astronomy at one explicit instant. Browse a complete bounded year with a circular overview and a daily slider. Distinguish solved astronomical events, sampled changes, projected computus and published observances.
3. Compare selected religious and esoteric texts by what they actually date, their editions and interpretive assumptions. An undated text must stay undated.
4. Browse the previously audited twenty-eight historical election/conflict records for seven country observers, with marked events, source wording, outcomes and three assumed clocks. Preserve shared events, alternative date conventions, unavailable values and the original descriptive analysis.

## Research contract

The Newton analysis/synthesis and Tesla mechanism/resonance skills guide reconstruction and skeptical checks; their authors are not simulated panel members. The six review roles are AI agents, not human tradition authorities or observed research participants. Newton's theology, astrology, civil calendars, astronomy and modern statistical inference remain separate claim types. No complete reading of every source book is claimed.

A civil-date mapping cannot identify a government's gods, rituals or hidden intentions. Country dates are observations, not evidence of a unique decision rule. This work does not supply attack windows, opponent vulnerability rankings or future war predictions. Historical outcomes are source facts, not a fitted success target.

Round 1 diagnoses the published baseline and produces the implementation. Round 2 reviews that changed artifact and implements corrections. Round 3 verifies the revised candidate, fixes remaining blockers and records actual release evidence. Root owns integration, MCP, navigation, generators and publication. Finding/decision/result records are retained in the panel ledger rather than counting repeated reports as product iterations.

## Release contract

Run the repository's five gates, new semantic and transport checks, existing browser journeys and the complete horizon stress gate. Keep unknown event clocks explicit and do not relax inherited assertions. Publish only the tested Git candidate, then verify the production release identity and live routes. Static Pages publication is distinct from a hosted MCP service, provider inference or Supabase deployment.
