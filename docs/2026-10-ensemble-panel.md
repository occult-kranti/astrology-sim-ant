Baseline: `3be3d464a718d4562d3d1eb0254f90b706a7327f`, branch `feat/mobile-calendar-observatory`. These are six independent AI-agent roles and a root moderator. They are not historical authors, human practitioners, observed participants, trained specialist models or licensed cultural/religious authorities.
