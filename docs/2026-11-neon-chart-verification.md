## Review and checks

Six AI engineering perspectives cover integration, palette, chart rendering, traditional/drawing renderers, verification and independent review. The first review captured fourteen baseline images from the exact published source. It identified a cream Sigil canvas, weak enabled export controls, flat small markers and dim unselected/traditional lines. The second review captured fourteen after images and confirmed a visible improvement.

Raster contrast testing then found some neon glyphs had only 3–4.2:1 contrast against their new shaded discs. The renderer added a crisp native SVG theme-background outline. Verification retains the original 4.5:1 text threshold and accepts the outline as backing only when its actual computed paint is opaque, precedes the fill and spans at least one displayed CSS pixel. The final neon suite passes 192 checks over five journeys. Its thirteen actual screenshots include 320px, forced-color and print captures; nineteen actual downloads retain the visible output. Tested glyph text reaches at least 7.15:1 contrast, with native outlines spanning at least one displayed CSS pixel. The updated Black suite retains all 239 checks and tests the semantic selected connector separately from its explicitly decorative light layer. Diagnostic runs remain separate from final acceptance.

Actual standalone SVG/PNG checks verify black canvases, resolved gradient colors, valid local paint/clip references, actual raster output, held selection/geometry and unchanged canonical JSON. Independent review verified both frozen drivers, 24 screenshots and 30 compressed/expanded downloads across the two chart gates. All six downloaded SVGs have local resolved definitions, and the original Moon phase implementation and metadata remain unchanged. The native PNG exporter now captures the visible theme background before asynchronous rasterization: Black corners are opaque RGB(0,0,0), while the tested Parchment ground remains RGB(246,241,231). The existing Black and site-theme gates remain in the Pages workflow; a bounded five-journey neon gate covers Atlas at 390/1440px, shared Timeline, the traditional Workbench and Sigil previews.

The checks use emulated Chromium viewports and scripted keyboard/paint assertions. They do not establish physical-device usability, screen-reader compatibility, exhaustive case coverage or photographic accuracy of planetary surfaces. The [Pages workflow](../.github/workflows/pages.yml) retains all inherited release gates; its artifacts and deployed `release.json` bind publication to the tested commit.
