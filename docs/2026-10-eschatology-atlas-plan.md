## What the visitor can do

Choose a claim, inspect its source and provenance, trace typed relationships in a spider/network view, and compare source dates with claimed target dates on a timeline and table. The selected claim synchronizes these views. A full text alternative, search, filters, sorting and keyboard controls make the graph optional rather than the only route to the evidence.

An explicit study instant can be inspected through the thirteen existing civil calendar conventions and the three implemented Lilly, Agrippa and Jyotiṣa reading lenses. These are different forms of output: a civil calendar does not automatically have a validated astrological interpretation. A partial date, unknown date or alleged document date is never silently converted into an authenticated event instant. The existing 1900–2100 calculation range remains explicit.

Newton's source-bound 2060 conditional lower bound remains separate from his different 2370 upper-bound argument. Neither supplies a precise modern day. A visitor can deliberately choose a study day without converting it into a prophetic date.

## Evidence questions

| Question | Distinguishing record | Current research rule |
|---|---|---|
| Is a particular version of the alleged Pike three-world-wars letter authenticated? | Traceable manuscript, custodian record or provenance-supported contemporary publication | Later quotations and repeated websites do not establish the missing original; preserve version differences and attribution |
| Does the inspected text specify a target date? | Exact passage giving that date, with edition and locator | Keep document, publication, statement, historical-context and target dates separate |
| Does an edge establish a historical relationship? | Citation, documented transmission or another precisely supported relation | Analyst comparison edges are identified as comparisons; layout, similarity and adjacency are not evidence of covert association |
| What does a named tradition actually teach? | Its particular text or institution's statement, with scope and variants | Freemasonry, Jewish/Christian/Hermetic Kabbalah, ancient Baal traditions and modern Satanic movements are not one system or all secret societies |
| Do matching calendar labels corroborate a prediction? | Independent observations and a declared test outside the selection process | Conversions describe an instant; no prophecy probability, national belief classifier or future attack ranking is produced |

The investigative-evidence skill governs source identity, item integrity, claim status and evidence dependence. The panel-led workflow uses three actual implementation/review rounds. These are six AI review roles, not human religious authorities or observed participant research.

## Ownership

- Pike/Masonic source reviewer: a new source/claim ledger and bounded acquisition record.
- Comparative reviewer: selected Kabbalah, Baal and modern Satanic sources, with Newton context and explicit category differences.
- Engine owner: strict catalogue/graph/date-role contract, canonical sampled frame and named readings, exports and focused tests.
- Interface owner: new page, synchronized graph/timeline/table/inspector, responsive layout and state recovery.
- Independent verifier: source/math checks, adversarial date precision, real downloads, graphs, keyboard and browser evidence.
- Release/statistical reviewer: prior release completion, then independent cross-checks and artifact review.

Root owns integration, navigation, MCP, generators, documentation and publication. Existing Newton/country pages and calculators remain unchanged unless a demonstrated regression requires a correction. Source files are not harvested into a training corpus merely because they are readable online.

## Acceptance

Graph nodes and edges must resolve to their claims/sources; graph geometry has no causal meaning. Unknown and partial dates remain visible and exportable. Verified exact days, explicit study choices, unavailable epochs and missing local clocks have different states. Source-only selections cannot retain a previous chart link. Replacing/cancelling work must preserve one coherent displayed context and disable stale exports. Actual downloads must match the held canonical output.

Run the repository gates, dedicated independent/browser checks and actual MCP transport checks. Retain prior release gates and the horizon stress job. Publish only after exact-head CI; verify deployed release identity and the new route before claiming it is live.
