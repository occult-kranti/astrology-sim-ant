How to read an inverse chronological argument

Separate the source’s symbolic expression, its interpretation as a duration, the proposed historical anchor, and the arithmetic result. Reversing a sum can recover an assumed anchor; it does not independently establish that the anchor is historically or theologically correct.

A symbolic 360-day expression is not a claim that the astronomical solar year contains 360 days. Numerical agreement across conversions also does not supply independent support.
